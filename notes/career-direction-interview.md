## Why this exists

Before committing to a job-search direction, the user wants to be interviewed to clarify what matters most in their next role. The core decision to resolve:

- **Continue in consulting**, OR
- **Push hard on startups / entrepreneurship** (founding or early-employee), OR
- **Join a product company** (IC or leadership track at an established product org)

## How to run the interview

Conduct it conversationally, one topic at a time — not a questionnaire dump. Probe for concrete examples ("tell me about a time when…") rather than abstract preferences. Suggested areas to cover:

1. **Energy audit** — Which past projects/engagements energized vs drained them? What was the common thread?
2. **Autonomy vs structure** — Do they thrive with blank-page ambiguity or with defined scope and support?
3. **Risk tolerance** — Financial runway, family/life obligations, appetite for equity-vs-cash tradeoffs.
4. **Craft vs leverage** — Go deep on the craft, or maximize impact through others (team, product, company)?
5. **Time horizon** — Optimizing for the next 2 years, 5 years, or a 10+ year arc?
6. **Consulting pros/cons** — What specifically is working and not working about consulting right now?
7. **Startup appetite** — Founder vs early employee? Solo vs co-founder? Have they validated a specific idea?
8. **Product company fit** — What kind of company (stage, domain, culture)? IC path or management?
9. **Deal-breakers and non-negotiables** — Location, comp floor, remote policy, domain, people.
10. **Identity / story** — How do they want to describe their work in 5 years? What would make them proud?
